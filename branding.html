FAQ

Brand questions, answered.

A few quick answers before you send the first message.

Do I need a full rebrand?

Often not. Many businesses just need direction and consistency. We'll tell you honestly what is worth doing.

Can you do brand and website together?

Yes, and it usually works best that way, so the identity is built straight into the live site.

Will I get files I can use?

Yes. You receive logo files, a colour and type system and simple guidelines.

What if I already have a logo?

We can build the wider system around it, or refine it where that helps.
