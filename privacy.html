Privacy

Privacy Policy.

This page explains how Made to Scale handles information submitted through the contact form or direct email. Last updated 2 May 2026.

Contact the studio

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, business name, selected services, referral source, and project message.

How we use it

Your information is used to respond to your enquiry, understand the project, prepare a quote, and communicate about potential work.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. If a project requires selected collaborators, only relevant project information is shared where needed.

Retention

Enquiry information may be kept for reasonable business records, follow-up, and project planning unless you ask for it to be removed.

Analytics and cookies

This first version does not require analytics cookies. If analytics are added later, this page should be updated with the tools and choices available.

Your choices

You can ask to access, update, or delete enquiry information by contacting tomarasg@icloud.com.
