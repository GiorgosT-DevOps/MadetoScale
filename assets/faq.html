General

Scope, price, and timing.

Quotes are shaped around the project scope, goals, content, timeline, and support needs.

How much does a website cost?

Every website is quoted based on pages, content, visual design needs, functionality, integrations, timeline, and support requirements.

Do you show prices publicly?

No. The studio understands the project first, then gives a tailored quote that reflects the real scope.

How long does a project take?

A simple website can move faster. Web apps, booking systems, brand work, and AI integrations need more planning and testing.

Can I update the website myself?

That depends on the build. If editable content is important, it can be planned into the scope with a CMS or structured update process.

Working model

Small studio does not mean improvised.

The studio is intentionally direct, but the process stays structured.

Who will work on my project?

Selected collaborators can be added when a project needs extra expertise.

Do you work with all industries?

Yes. The studio is open to different industries and adapts the solution to each business goal.

Can we work remotely?

Yes. Communication can happen through email, calls, shared notes, and project updates.

What if I do not know exactly what I need?

Start with the project planner or a short message. The first job is shaping the problem clearly.

Support and AI

After launch, and beyond static pages.

Maintenance and AI integrations are planned around practical value, not novelty.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes. Care plans can cover updates, fixes, content changes, performance checks, small improvements, and future feature work.

Can you add AI to an existing website?

Yes. AI chatbots, automated lead replies, content tools, internal automations, and custom features can be explored.

Can you improve an existing site?

Yes. Depending on the platform and current quality, improvements can include speed, structure, content, UX, forms, and conversion paths.

Do you offer booking systems?

Yes. Booking flows can be planned for appointments, reservations, services, customer enquiries, or internal operations.
