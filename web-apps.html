FAQ

Web app questions, answered.

A few quick answers before you send the first message.

Is a web app overkill for my business?

Often a small focused tool saves hours every week. We help you decide whether an app is worth it before committing.

Can it connect to tools I already use?

Usually yes, through their APIs. We confirm what is possible during planning.

Do you handle logins and accounts?

Yes. We can build authentication-ready flows so the right people see the right data.

What happens to my data?

It stays yours. We agree where it lives and how it is handled before we build.
