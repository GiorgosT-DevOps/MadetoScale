FAQ

Care questions, answered.

A few quick answers before you send the first message.

Do I have to be on a plan?

No. We offer care plans for convenience, but one-off fixes and updates are also fine.

What if you didn't build my site?

Often we can still help. We'll review the site first and tell you honestly what care is realistic.

How fast are fixes?

Urgent issues are prioritised. We agree response expectations as part of the plan.

Can I change or cancel?

Yes. Care should keep earning its place, so plans stay flexible.
